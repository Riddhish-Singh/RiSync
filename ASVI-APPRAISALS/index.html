Eligibility Rules & References

Eligibility Rules

Fully Eligible: DOJ on or before 30 Sep 2025 (6 months prior to 31 Mar 2026)

Pro-Rata Eligible: DOJ between 01 Oct 2025 – 31 Mar 2026 (months worked / 12)

Not Eligible: DOJ after 31 Mar 2026
